**First public pre-release of Pebble** — a desktop companion for Windows that understands English, हिंदी and Hinglish, with an AI that runs entirely on your laptop. Early software: please report anything odd via *About → Report a bug* or *Pebble misunderstood me*.

## Install
1. Download **`Pebble-0.1.2.msi`** below. The models are inside, so nothing else to download.
2. Optional: check it in PowerShell with `Get-FileHash .\Pebble-0.1.2.msi` and compare it with `SHA256SUMS`.
3. Run it. It installs for your user only, no admin needed. If Windows says *"Windows protected your PC"*, click **More info → Run anyway** (the installer isn't code-signed yet).
4. Start Pebble from the Start menu. **Ctrl+Alt+Space** to type to it, **hold** it to talk.

Requirements: Windows 10/11 x64, ~1 GB disk, 8 GB RAM recommended, a microphone for voice.

## What's in it
- **Desktop pet** on your taskbar: 5 characters with moods. It sleeps when you're away, and stays out of the way of fullscreen apps and videos (YouTube, Netflix, Prime, Hotstar, VLC…).
- **Talk or type** in English, Devanagari or Hinglish:
  - reminders ("kal shaam 7 baje mummy ko call", "friday wali meeting 4 baje")
  - notes, water, questions, small talk
  - a conversation view in Quick Add, and a Chat page with your history
- **Understands you locally:**
  - **Commands:** a 31 MB model, ~5 ms per command.
  - **Speech:** Dolphin for Hindi/Hinglish (~0.1–0.2 s for a short command), Whisper for English.
  - **When unsure, it asks**, and "Not what I meant" undoes any guess.
- **Calm reminders:** water, stretch and eye breaks. They back off when you skip, have a "Less often" button, and learn which times work for you.
- **Grows with you:** Teen, Adult and Legendary are earned through tasks (reminders done, water goals, active days, chats).
- **Private by design:**
  - no account, no cloud, no analytics, no network connections
  - microphone only while you hold the key, with a switch to turn it off
  - see [PRIVACY.md](https://github.com/Wickedsoni/pebble/blob/main/PRIVACY.md)

## Measured (installed build, Core Ultra 9 laptop)
- **Idle CPU:** ~0.02%.
- **Memory:** ~140 MB with the pet only. ~580 MB while the speech models are loaded; they free themselves after 10 idle minutes (back to ~230 MB).
- **Hindi speech on FLEURS (30 clips):** 16% character error rate on clean audio.

## Known limits
- Small-talk replies are short built-in lines; real conversation needs the planned local chat model.
- "Start with Windows" hasn't been tested on many machines yet.
- Windows only.

Models for building from source: [models-2026.10](https://github.com/Wickedsoni/pebble/releases/tag/models-2026.10).
